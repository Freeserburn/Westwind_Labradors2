AKC registration

Registration with clarity.

All dogs owned by Westwind Labradors will be AKC registered. When a litter is born, we plan to register that litter with the American Kennel Club; new owners will then complete individual puppy registration.

Puppies are initially placed with limited AKC registration. Full AKC registration with breeding rights may be available at an additional cost only when specifically designated.
